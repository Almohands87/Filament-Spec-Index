How to read this

Values are manufacturer datasheet typicals normalised to XY tensile strength (ISO 527), not design allowables. Bambu Lab's public guide publishes bending strength; those figures are converted at roughly 1.45× and shown separately with the original test method labelled.

Tg and Tm come from DSC data where a manufacturer publishes it, otherwise from polymer literature. A blank Tm means the polymer is amorphous and has no melting point — it softens progressively above Tg.

The Z-axis figure is a derate, not a measurement. Layer adhesion depends on chamber temperature, layer height, nozzle material and speed as much as on the polymer. Print a coupon before trusting a load path.
